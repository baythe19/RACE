## 15. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: What makes RACE different from calling a local tow truck?
Local tow operators often do not have standard pricing, take 1-2 hours to arrive, and may use improper towing chains that damage your car's bumper or transmission. RACE provides guaranteed 30-minute arrival, 100% upfront fixed pricing, GPS live tracking, and verified operators with proper flatbed and wheel-lift machinery.

#### Q2: How quickly can a tow truck or technician reach me?
Our average response time is **25 minutes** in city limits and under **35 minutes** along major highway stretches. Because our recovery fleet is distributed strategically rather than parked in a single garage, the nearest unit is dispatched immediately.

#### Q3: Which type of tow truck do I need for my car?
If you have an Automatic transmission (AT, CVT, DSG), an All-Wheel Drive / 4x4 SUV, or a luxury vehicle, we strongly advise our **Hydraulic Flatbed Towing** where all four wheels rest safely on the truck platform. For short city tows or compact cars, our **Wheel-Lift Towing** is cost-effective and swift.

#### Q4: How do you verify your on-demand drivers?
Every RACE driver undergoes a stringent background verification process including police verification, valid commercial/private DL verification, reference checks, and a practical driving assessment.

#### Q5: Can I hire a driver to drive my automatic / luxury car?
Yes! Our drivers are trained and experienced with Manual, Automatic, AMT, DCT, Electric Vehicles (EV), and high-end luxury vehicles. You can specify your car model while booking.

#### Q6: Are there any hidden charges or night surcharges?
None. When you enter your pickup and drop location, the price shown is the final price. We believe in 100% transparency.

#### Q7: What if my car breaks down without internet connectivity?
You can dial our 24/7 direct helpline number **`+91 8249475731`**. Our phone dispatchers will locate you via landmark assistance and assign the closest unit manually.

#### Q8: How does the Vehicle QR sticker protect my privacy?
When placed on your windshield, any person or traffic officer can scan the code to send you a notification or call you via our secure system without ever seeing your actual phone number.
